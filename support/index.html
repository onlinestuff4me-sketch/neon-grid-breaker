How to play

Time only moves when you move.

Stand still and the world nearly stops. Move, and it catches up. Every shot fired at you is dodgeable if you use that.

Drag the right side to look, the left to walk.

Either thumb works — whichever lands first becomes the one that steers.

Tap anywhere to shoot.

Hold the hourglass to freeze time outright. It drains a bank you refill by killing.

Ammo is finite.

Enemies drop what they were carrying. Run dry and you fall back to the knife, which is when freezing time stops being a luxury.

Common problems

My scores disappeared

On the web, scores live in your browser's storage — clearing site data or using private browsing loses them. In the iOS app they are kept in the app's own storage and survive.

It runs slowly on my phone

Close other tabs and apps first. If it is still slow, email me your exact device model — the game turns some effects off automatically when frames run long, and I would like to know if that is not working.

No sound

Check the silent switch on the side of the phone, then the in-game pause menu. Browsers also require one tap before any audio can start.

The web version looks out of date

Pull to refresh. If it persists, remove it from your home screen and add it again.
