TIME SHATTER

Privacy

Last updated 14 August 2026

TIME SHATTER collects no data about you. None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What is stored

Your best scores, your recent runs, the doors you have cleared and the settings you have chosen are saved on your own device — in the browser's local storage on the web, and in the app's own storage on iOS. That information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, deletes it permanently.

What is sent

Nothing. The game does not talk to a server while you play. The web version downloads its own files the first time you visit and then runs entirely offline.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center and choose to submit a score, Apple handles that entirely — your score and your Game Center nickname go to Apple, not to us, and are governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can play the whole game without ever signing in.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page changes first, and the date above changes with it.
